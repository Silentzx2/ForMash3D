// The Pipeline

Eight stages. Zero quality shortcuts.

Every asset flows through an immutable high-fidelity master before any optimization touches a derivative.

01

Input

Image, text or scribble validated & framed

02

Preprocess

03

Generate

Model-aware routing across 10 generators

04

Master

Immutable high-fidelity source asset

05

Retopo + UV

FastMesh quads & PartUV atlases

06

PBR Bake

Albedo, normal, roughness, metallic, AO

07

LODs

08

Delivery
